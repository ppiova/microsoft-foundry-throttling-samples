Control each model call

ControlWhat to implementTradeoff

Pacing + queueSpace new sends; bound the queue and its wait time.Some work waits or is rejected

How do we verify the cause?

A useful diagnosis connects
the error to the workload

  1. Capture the responseStatus, service error, retry guidance and available limit headers.
  2. Describe the request patternArrival times, concurrency, prompt size and output allowance.
  3. Check the relevant deploymentProvider, model, region, deployment type and configured limits.
  4. Repeat with one controlled changeKeep enough evidence to compare the outcomes.

Using the strategies together

Prevention first, recovery second

StrategyRoleExpected effect
BurstBaseline or intentional traffic shapeHighest exposure to request-rate rejection
Retry with backoffRecover an eligible 429 within a budgetMay improve completion but adds attempts and time
Paced requestsSpace every send, including retriesReduces burst pressure but cannot guarantee zero 429s

Our Paced scenario combines pacing with bounded retries. Use Burst only when the workload and service can accept it.
